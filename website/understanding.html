WHAT I UNDERSTOOD LATER

Later...

Sometimes I couldn't sleep, because every night you were on my mind.

Class lo ninnu chusthu, I would have so many things to say.

But if I actually looked at you, words wouldn't come out.

Naake ardham kaaledu... why this was happening to me.

Later I thought, “Maybe girls are like this only.”

Few days later, I met another girl in the train.

Then I understood... all girls are not the same.

When I was little, while watching Adavari Matalaku Arthale Verule, I used to wonder why the actress was behaving that way.

After you rejected me, I understood her.

I started understanding you too.

From then, I didn't want to force you.

But I couldn't stop feeling what I felt.

I don't even know if I should call it love.
