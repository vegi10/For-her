THINGS I COULDN'T SAY

Things I
never said.

Class lo ninnu chusthu everything cheppali anipinchedi.

But I couldn't.

Phone call lo kuda cheppalenu.

Outside face-to-face ga kuda cheppalenu.

So I made this.

Not because I expect something from you.

Not because I want you to change your decision.

I just wanted you to know the things you never knew.

That's all.
